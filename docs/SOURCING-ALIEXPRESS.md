## How to use this list

1. Open each link, check the **current** price, shipping cost and delivery time **to Sweden**,
   seller rating, recent reviews with photos, and variants.
2. Shortlist 1–2 per category and **order samples to your own address**. Check fit on common
   Swedish furniture (e.g. IKEA kitchen fronts and drawers), adhesive strength and residue,
   materials, smell, packaging and instructions.
3. Message the seller (AliExpress chat) and ask for the documents in "Ask every supplier" below.
   No documents → don't list the product.
4. Take your own product photos from the samples (better for the brand, and avoids image-rights
   problems with seller photos).
5. Only then replace a `demo` draft with the real product (see PRODUCT-DATA.md).

Prefer clean, neutral designs (white, grey, transparent) that fit the Scandinavian look;
skip cartoon/animal designs.

Why lower priority: under Elsäkerhetsverket's ELSÄK-FS 2022:1, general-use outlets in fixed
installations must be child-protected ("petskyddade") or placed to limit the risk, so many
Swedish homes already have protected outlets. Demand is mainly older homes, extension leads and
power strips. Consider testing a **power-strip/cable safety box** instead. Never market socket
covers as making an installation "compliant" or "safe".

### 6. Startpaket

No ready-made kit worth recommending turned up – most "kits" are single-category packs.
Build your own bundles from the products above, **ideally from one seller** so the order ships
as one parcel. With separate AliExpress orders, the customer receives several parcels on
different days – say so honestly on the product page or avoid the bundle.

Example structure (prices to be set after sampling):
- *Startpaket Kök*: magnetic locks (4–8) + strap locks + fridge lock
- *Startpaket Vardagsrum*: transparent edge strip + corner protectors + socket covers
- *Startpaket Dörrar*: pinch guards + door lever lock

## Ask every supplier (copy into AliExpress chat)

> Hello! I'm considering selling this product to consumers in Sweden (EU). Can you send:
> 1. The EU Declaration of Conformity / CE documents, if the product requires CE.
> 2. Test reports from an accredited lab (materials and child-safety function).
> 3. Manufacturer name, postal address and contact, and your EU responsible person (GPSR).
> 4. Exact materials (including the adhesive) and dimensions.
> 5. Original instructions and warnings (we will translate them into Swedish).
> 6. Permission to use your product photos, or confirmation that we may take our own.
> 7. Can you ship from an EU warehouse? Typical delivery time to Sweden?
> 8. Can you ship several different items in one parcel, without invoices or ads in the package?

## Things that will matter more than price

- **EU product safety (GPSR, Regulation (EU) 2023/988, in force since 13 Dec 2024):** a product
  sold to EU consumers needs an EU-based economic operator (e.g. importer or authorised
  representative). Their name and contact details must be on the product/packaging and in the
  listing, with Swedish warnings. When you dropship from China under your own brand, you may
  carry importer-type obligations yourself. **Get advice on this before the first sale.**
- **Delivery time:** filter AliExpress by "Ships from" an EU country or by Choice offers where
  possible; only publish delivery estimates you have measured with samples.
- **Shopify fulfilment:** the usual route is the **DSers** app (AliExpress's official
  dropshipping partner). It imports products and places supplier orders. Product data then still
  needs the Swedish rewrite and verified metafields in this theme.
- **Margins:** price = (product + shipping to SE) + Shopify & payment fees + 25 % VAT + your
  margin + an allowance for returns and lost parcels. Very cheap items (under 10 kr cost) usually
  only work in bundles.
